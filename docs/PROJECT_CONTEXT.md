## 2. Project plan (from `Project Plan for AI - SBP Group 36 - HFTP.pdf`)

### Context
HFTP (Hospitality Financial and Technology Partners) is a US-based international nonprofit association, active for more than 70 years, serving finance and technology professionals in hospitality. AI is changing the industry, but uncertainty about implementation, financing and governance makes adoption hard. HFTP asked for research into how **European hotels and F&B operators can adopt, resource, govern and scale AI**. The main readers are **HFTP members** (industry and hospitality leaders making AI investment decisions).

The findings should help organisations:
- assess the current and future AI landscape in hospitality
- understand specific AI use cases
- judge their readiness and resource needs
- understand AI governance and how it is changing
- integrate AI into strategic planning

### Mission
To investigate how European hotels and F&B operators plan to adopt, resource, govern, and scale AI strategically, producing practical insights that help HFTP members make informed decisions about AI investment and implementation.

### Deliverables
| # | Deliverable | Content |
|---|---|---|
| D1 | European Hospitality AI Landscape | Current state of adoption, planned initiatives and investment, overview of focus countries, where AI sits in strategic planning |
| D2 | Operator Segmentation Analysis | Chains vs independents vs affiliated properties: priorities, ambitions, barriers, approaches |
| D3 | Market-Tier Comparison | Luxury vs midscale vs budget: priorities, barriers, approaches, expected use cases |
| D4 | AI Use-Case Catalogue | Guest-facing, operational, commercial (RM, sales & marketing), admin/back-office; maturity and adoption level of each |
| D5 | AI Decision-Making Framework | Identifying opportunities, evaluation criteria (value, complexity, risk, integration, readiness), scoring, go/no-go tool |
| D6 | AI Resource & Readiness Assessment | People/skills, financial investment, tech infrastructure, data, organisational readiness, self-assessment checklist |
| D7 | European AI Governance Briefing | EU AI Act plus Swiss and UK rules; relevance to selecting, deploying and managing AI |
| D8 | AI Strategic Planning Framework | Moving from isolated projects to strategy, investment and operating models |
| D9 | HFTP Hospitality AI Insight Series | Articles, insights, frameworks and recommendations for HFTP channels |
| D10 | HFTP Industry Engagement Content | Presentation materials, conference topics, panel themes, executive talking points |

### Scope limits
- 5–7 European countries
- Hotel operations, including F&B inside hotels; standalone F&B operators excluded

### Communication plan
- **Coach:** weekly in-person meeting, Thursdays 15:30–16:30; agenda at least 24h before; minutes within 24h
- **Client:** weekly online meeting plus a WhatsApp group; agenda at least 24h before; minutes within 24h (coach in copy)
- **Scope changes:** must be validated in writing by client, coach and team

### Research methodology (summary)
- **Secondary:** desk research, industry databases (e.g. CoStar, STR), official websites, industry reports and statistics
- **Primary interviews:** hospitality group CIOs/CEOs/GMs, hospitality and AI consultants, AI solution providers/startups, regulators, cybersecurity experts, field operators
- Governance research covers the EU AI Act, data protection, payment security and local regulations by country
