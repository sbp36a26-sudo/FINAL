## Revision list vs booklet (checked 17 Nov 2026)

Reflected in the booklet: Annex I safety-component carve-out and the lifts / gas / pressure equipment remainder (C6), human sign-off does not change classification, agency staff and freelancers in scope, working hours and leave decisions, AI-driven non-renewal of fixed-term contracts (C1), high-risk dates 2 Dec 2027 / 2 Aug 2028, SME/SMC fine caps, marking deadline 2 Dec 2026.

Not found in the booklet text: Art. 4a (special-category data for bias testing), Art. 27 FRIA, Art. 25(4) open-source carve-out, AI Office powers (Arts. 75a–75d), Art. 99(1) warnings / non-monetary penalties, Art. 17(2) QMS proportionality, Art. 60a, Art. 2(13). These may have been left out on purpose for a non-specialist booklet; the explorer's data layer covers them.
