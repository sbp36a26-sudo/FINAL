The earlier version (17 Nov 2026, 26 pages) stays as `AI_Governance_for_European_Hotels.pdf`.

## What it is
D7 booklet for hotel owners, head offices, GMs and department heads: what the EU AI Act (as amended by the Digital Omnibus,
Regulation (EU) 2026/1744) asks of them. Part A for everyone, then a page per role (B), per department (C), Switzerland and UK (D),
"What is still moving", glossary, 60 references (APA 7). Marks: (draft guidance), (our reading), (contested), (proposal). Tags for
other laws: [GDPR] [Labour] [Consumer] [Competition] [Liability] [Cyber/PCI] [CH] [UK]. Not legal advice.

## Key content
- Start here, five things to do now: list every AI tool (GM; head office pools lists); chatbots and phone agents say they are AI
  (since 2 Aug 2026); stop CVs or staff data pasted into general chatbots to rank people; ask rota, housekeeping, call-centre and
  camera vendors about ranking staff, emotions, face or voice recognition and switch off what is not needed; brief staff who use AI
  and keep a note (AI literacy, since 2 Feb 2025). Labour: works council first (DE, FR can stop a rollout).
- Five labels: Prohibited (from 2 Feb 2025), High-risk (from 2 Dec 2027), High-risk if… (2 Dec 2027; regulated products 2 Aug 2028),
  Transparency (2 Aug 2026), Minimal; plus "outside the high-risk list" (e.g. face check-in, draft guidance).
- Maker vs user; four routes to becoming the maker: build, brand, change, repurpose. Seven vendor questions (A3).
- Who is the user: owner-operated, managed (management company), franchised (franchisee), leased (tenant) (A4, our reading).
- Dates (A5): 2 Feb 2025, 2 Aug 2026, 9 Dec 2026 (Product Liability), 2 Dec 2027, 11 Dec 2027 (Cyber Resilience Act), 2 Aug 2028.
- Exposure (A6): of 91 uses, 2 high-risk as normally used, 14 more high-risk depending on set-up (11 of the 16 concern staff,
  six departments), 5 can become prohibited through one feature, 11 must say they are AI or label realistic images.
- Fines (A7): EUR 35m/7%; 15m/3%; 7.5m/1%; GDPR 20m/4% and 10m/2%; CH FADP up to CHF 250,000 on the individual.
- Department pages C1 to C6 (HR holds most high-risk AI), Switzerland (no AI act; FADP applies; AI bill consultation spring 2027) and UK
  (no AI act; UK GDPR as amended by the Data (Use and Access) Act 2025).
