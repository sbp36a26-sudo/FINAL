# Hotel AI readiness tool

The 91 use cases of the hotel AI catalogue, each with the resources it needs in six areas, and a readiness check that tells a hotel whether it can pilot a tool, scale it, or should build foundations first.

Most hotel AI tools stand on the same foundations. What changes from one tool to the next is the data, the system links, the hardware and the legal risk.

The building on the right is the framework behind this tool. A hotel builds the ground floor once. Each new AI tool then needs some of the middle floor and the top-floor pack for its type.

1. Browse or search the 91 catalogue use cases by department, type of tool or resource intensity.
2. **See what it needs**Open a use case: the resources it needs in people, skills, money, technology, data and organisation.
3. Six resource checks plus the D6 checklist questions that matter for that type of tool.
4. **Read the result and fix the gaps**Build foundations first, ready to pilot, or ready to scale, with the gaps listed in the order to fix them.

## Why start from readiness, not from the budget

In the HES-SO survey of 1,485 European hotels, high setup cost is now named by about a third of responding hotels, and poor knowledge of the solutions on offer is the most cited barrier. Fewer than half of hotel company leaders trust their data, and few chains can show a return. Each figure comes from a different survey: read it with its base.
