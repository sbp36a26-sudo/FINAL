# "Do you need AI?" v2.5: session kit for the timed manager test

Council BUILD NOW 12 (`reports/Council - Do you need AI v2.4.md`, section 5 (c)). Everything below comes from that section; only the consent line is new. Usability test only: no figure from these sessions goes into a deliverable; quotes only with permission.

## Script (one page, read by the lead; no help during the task)

1. **Setup.** 30 minutes. The tester's own laptop or a team laptop, the hub tab "Do you need AI?". Sharing is off in v2.5, so nothing the tester answers leaves the browser. Two team members: one leads (reads this script), one notes (time stamps, words, where they hesitate), as in the interviews.
2. **Consent line (read aloud).** "We are testing a student prototype, not you. My colleague notes your words and the time you take. Your answers stay in this browser and are not sent anywhere. We will quote you only if you say we may."
3. **Task 1.** "Think of one problem in your hotel." Let them go through the tool to the result.
4. **Task 2.** The same tool with one fixed use case for comparison: gx-2 (AI chat assistant for guests).
5. **After 30 seconds on the result page, ask:** "What does the score tell you?" "What does the verdict tell you?" "What would you do on Monday?" "Can you start now?"
6. **The PDF.** Ask them to download it and read their action plan aloud.
7. **Close.** "Would you forward this, and to whom?"

Testers: five managers. Mix: two GMs of independents, one group or chain property manager, one head of department, one IT or finance person.

## Pass line (team judgement)

4 of 5 finish in 20 minutes; 4 of 5 describe the score as "my own answers, not a measured grade"; no tester reads a verdict as legal clearance; fewer than one in five tailored answers is "Don't know yet". Below that line, change the tool before any HFTP member sees it.

## Early warning signs (council, risks to watch)

- A tester says "94, that's good" on "Not ready yet", or asks "what is a good score?"
- More than a third of must-have answers in a session are "Planned"; testers say "well, we plan to".
- A tester skips the EU AI Act fold, asks "so we can go ahead?", or nobody can say what a pilot is.
