# Readiness check audit, phase 1: facts and logic

*20 Nov 2026. Read-only audit of `tools/readiness-check/` (rules v3, build of 20 Nov 2026) and the data it embeds. No project file was changed.*

## How this was checked

- **Code and copy:** read `src/logic.js`, `src/check.js`, `src/check.html`, `src/team.js`, `src/team.html`, `src/store.js`, `build.mjs`, `README.md`.
- **Logic:** loaded `src/logic.js` in Node the way `tests/logic.test.mjs` does and ran all 98 tools × 3 hotel types, plus about 30 adversarial answer sets. Every "Repro" below is an actual run.
- **Law:** AI Act and Digital Omnibus claims checked against the Official Journal PDFs in `tools/ai-act-explorer/` (Regulation (EU) 2024/1689 and Regulation (EU) 2026/1744, OJ 24.7.2026). GDPR Art. 35 checked against the legislation.gov.uk copy of the EU text. EUR-Lex returned empty pages to curl, so nothing was read there.
- **Not verified at source:** Swiss Ordinance 3 Art. 26 and BGE 130 II 425 (fedlex needs JavaScript), the ICO guidance, the French CSE statement, the AEPD position, Cyber Resilience Act date. These rest on my own knowledge only.
- The existing tests pass (34 logic, 70 flow). None of them covers the faults below.

## Numbers the copy states, against what the tool does

| Stated | Actual (98 tools × 3 hotel types) |
|---|---|
| "N questions, about 15 minutes" (same time for every tool) | N runs from **17 to 33** (mean 22.9). Tick lists count as one question but hold 4–10 items, so there are 17–35 answer clicks plus 6–10 typed proof lines |
| "The N must-haves" | **6 to 10** (6: 18 tools, 7: 53, 8: 22, 9: 2, 10: 3) |
| "six short steps" | One step holds up to 10 questions (Organisation & governance, `fo-4`, managed hotel) |
| 31 D6 questions | P6 and O5 are **never asked** for any tool or hotel type; F5 is asked for 1 tool, T4 for 5 |

## Checked and found correct

- High-risk duties for Annex III uses apply from **2 Dec 2027**; product-embedded AI from 2 Aug 2028; new bans from 2 Dec 2026; Omnibus published 24.7.2026 and in force on the third day after (Regulation (EU) 2026/1744, Art. 113 as amended, local OJ PDF).
- "No duty under the AI Act beyond AI literacy" for minimal tools: Art. 4 as amended still binds users ("shall take measures to support the development of AI literacy").
- Logs kept at least six months; staff and representatives told before use; verification excluded from the biometric category; recruitment and staff monitoring on the high-risk list (2024/1689, Art. 26(6), 26(7), Annex III points 1 and 4).
- The chatbot notice is treated as the vendor's duty that the hotel checks, which matches Art. 50(1).
- All 21 named EU countries in the list are member states.
- No mention of AIHA or Wikipedia in the embedded data or screen copy.
- Thresholds: no rounding case exists where a bar shows "75%" but is treated as below 75%.
- Tests: 34 logic and 70 flow tests pass, as the README says.
