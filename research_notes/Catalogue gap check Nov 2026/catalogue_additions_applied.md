Competition law matters if other hotels' non-public data is used; consumer law if a matched price is personalised."
- **`gm-7` register label:** base minimal; questions: c_staff, c_biometric (soft), c_credit (soft), c_chat (soft), paste_data; provision: Art. 3(3); Art. 25. Reason shown: "Depends on what is built. Plain software without AI inside, such as a data export, is not an AI system. Data protection and security stay with the hotel either way."
